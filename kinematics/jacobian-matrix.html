Geometric Jacobian Matrix

\(\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf v\\\boldsymbol\omega\end{bmatrix} =\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix}J_v\\J_\omega\end{bmatrix}}_{J(\mathbf q)} \dot{\mathbf q}\)

Each column represents one joint's contribution to end-effector linear and angular velocity.

You can also select a column by clicking its header or a joint name.

Joint Rates

The green arrow shows the resultant.

The arrows share a visual scale that adapts to the model. Their directions and relative lengths are meaningful; velocity is measured in model units/s.

Jv: linear velocity Jω: angular velocity

The Jacobian uses revolute rates in rad/s and prismatic rates in model units/s. DH angles are edited in degrees.
